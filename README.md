## Purpose
The Mercury macropad is a 9+1 key macro pad aimed at being useful for a variety of purposes, the specifications of which is entirely up to the one who makes it. The design is centered around a nice!nano and nice!view, as well as Hotswap Kalih MX switches with Cherry MX stabilizer footprints. Further information can be found in [Bill of Materials](BillOfMaterials.txt).
